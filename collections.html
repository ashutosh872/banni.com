Fit & Measurement Guide

Size Guide

Finding your perfect fit is easy. All measurements are in inches and taken on the body — not the garment. When in doubt, size up.

Size Chest (in) Waist (in) Hip (in) Kurti Length (in)
XS 32" 32" 34" 44"
S 34" 34" 36" 44"
M 36" 36" 38" 44"
L 38" 38" 40" 45"
XL 40" 40" 42" 45"
XXL 42" 42" 44" 46"

How to Measure?

• Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping tape parallel to the floor.

• Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your torso.

• Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 8 inches below waist.

• Kurti Length: Measured from highest shoulder point straight down to the bottom hem.
